Thailand Province Thai Translation

Automatically translate Thailand's 77 provinces to Thai with the official "จ." prefix

Standardize Your Thai Addresses

Automatically update English province names to official Thai names for local compliance and professional reports

Standard Odoo deployments come with English names for Thailand's provinces, which can be inconsistent for local Thai business documents and tax reporting.

This module automatically updates all 77 provinces to their official Thai names with the standard "จ." prefix (e.g., จ.นนทบุรี). It ensures full compliance with Thai address standards.
